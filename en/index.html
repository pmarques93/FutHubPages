Privacy Policy — FutHub

Last updated: 7 August 2026

FutHub is an Android app that shows live football scores, statistics and broadcast channels. This policy explains the data the app processes and your rights.

1. No account

FutHub has no registration or sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address or account identifier. Your selected club, favourites, notification preferences and app language are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

2. Notifications

When you enable match notifications, your device subscribes to a Firebase Cloud Messaging topic (Google). Firebase processes a technical device identifier to deliver alerts. We do not associate it with your identity. You can disable notifications in the app or system settings.

3. Match data

The app reads match information from Firebase Firestore (Google). These reads do not contain your personal data.

4. Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics (Google) to understand aggregate app use and improve FutHub. It may process device information, language, technical identifiers and usage events. We do not use this data to personally identify you.

5. Advertising

FutHub displays ads through Google AdMob. Google may process the advertising identifier, IP address and usage data to serve and, where permitted, personalise ads. In the European Economic Area, we request consent before personalised ads where applicable; you can manage it in the app’s privacy options.

6. Contacting us

The Contact screen prepares a message and hands it to your device’s email app; FutHub does not send or store it. If you send it, we use your email address and message only to reply or address the reported issue, do not use them for advertising or share them with third parties, and delete the exchange when no longer needed.

7. Third parties

Google provides Firebase and AdMob. See its Privacy Policy, Firebase Privacy and AdMob privacy information.

8. Your rights

EU residents have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability. As we do not maintain an identified-user database, most rights concern Google’s processing or messages you send us.

9. Children and retention

FutHub is not directed at children under 13. Local data stays on your device until you remove it or uninstall the app; Google data is retained under Google’s policies; contact messages are deleted when no longer needed.

10. Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will publish the current dated version here. Contact pmiguelrmarquesdev@gmail.com with privacy questions.
